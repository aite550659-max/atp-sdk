## How Blockchains Are Being Used for AI

### 1. **Compute Marketplaces**
- Aggregating idle GPU capacity globally
- 70-90% cheaper than centralized cloud
- Permissionless access to compute
- Example: Akash, Render, io.net

### 2. **Model Training Data**
- Tokenized datasets for AI training
- Data provenance and licensing on-chain
- Fair compensation for data contributors
- Example: Ocean Protocol

### 3. **AI Agent Economies**
- Agents with wallets that can transact autonomously
- Agent-to-agent payments and coordination
- Verifiable agent identities
- Example: Fetch.ai Agentverse, Virtuals

### 4. **Verifiable AI Outputs**
- Proving AI model outputs are authentic
- Tracking AI decision provenance
- Tamper-proof audit trails
- Example: Hedera HCS for AI accountability

### 5. **Incentive Mechanisms for AI Development**
- Token rewards for contributing compute, data, or models
- Decentralized model evaluation
- Open source AI development incentives
- Example: Bittensor TAO emissions

### 6. **Proof of Humanity**
- Distinguishing humans from AI/bots
- Sybil resistance in the age of AI
- Human-only access to services
- Example: World (Worldcoin)
