### Content Formats by Performance

Based on analysis of successful accounts:

**High Engagement Formats:**
1. **Hot takes** — Contrarian opinions that invite debate
2. **Quote tweets with spicy commentary** — Add value, don't just amplify
3. **Behind-the-scenes** — Personal, authentic moments
4. **Data/insights with takeaways** — Jessica's style: stats + "let's go 👀"
5. **Threads** — Deep dives on topics (Balaji style for complex ideas)
6. **Questions** — Invite conversation

**Lower Engagement (Use Sparingly):**
- Links without commentary
- Generic "great post!" replies
- Pure promotional content
- Long blocks of text without hooks

### Tweet Length Guidelines

| Type | Length | Example |

| **Threads** | 5-12 tweets | Hook → Context → Insight → CTA |
| **Replies** | 1-3 sentences | Add value or ask a question |

*Goal: 500 followers*

4. **Build email list/newsletter** for deeper engagement
5. **Media appearances** — Podcasts, articles

## Part 4: Engagement Playbook

### Reply Strategy

**Do:**
- Add a new angle or insight
- Ask a smart follow-up question
- Share relevant experience/data
- Use humor when appropriate
- Tag other relevant people to expand conversation

**Don't:**
- "Great thread!" (empty)
- Copy their take in different words
- Be confrontational without substance
- Reply to everything (looks desperate)

### Quote Tweet Formula

```
[Your take/reaction] + [Why it matters or counterpoint]
Optional: Question to spark replies
Emoji for personality
```

**Examples:**
- "This is why audit trails matter. When AI agents control capital, 'trust me bro' isn't an architecture. 🔮"
- "Counterpoint: the problem isn't AI agency, it's unverifiable AI agency. Big difference. ⛓️"

### Handling Engagement

| Scenario | Response |
|----------|----------|
| **Positive reply** | Thank briefly, add value, or ask question |
| **Thoughtful disagreement** | Engage genuinely, acknowledge good points |
| **Bad faith attack** | Ignore or one witty response, then disengage |
| **Question** | Answer helpfully, link to resources |
| **Big account notices you** | Don't fanboy — stay in character, add value |

| **Getting into flame wars** | One response max, then disengage |
